How to play

Drag the three pieces from the tray onto the 8×8 board. Fill a whole row or column to clear it. When all three pieces are placed, you get three new ones. The game ends when none of your pieces fit.

Common questions

Can every round be finished? Yes. Every set of three pieces is checked before you get it, so there is always a way to place all three. Play carefully and you can keep going forever.

What is a Restore? Clearing lines and building combos fills the Restore meter (the ring around the heart). A full meter gives you a Restore. If you run out of moves, a Restore resets the current round so you can try it again, and you keep your score. You can hold up to 5.

What is the ⚡ boost? Once you have a high score, new games score faster through the first part of it, so you get back to your best sooner.

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your iPhone. Close the app any time and tap Continue to pick up where you left off.
