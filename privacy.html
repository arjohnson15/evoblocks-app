Privacy Policy

EvoBlocks for iPhone · Effective November 8, 2026

The short version

EvoBlocks does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no accounts.

What stays on your device

Your best score, your game in progress, and your settings (such as vibration) are saved only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not connect to any server, does not use third-party SDKs, and does not use advertising identifiers or location.

Children

EvoBlocks is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email support@evoplayer.us.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
